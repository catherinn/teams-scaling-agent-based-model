Team Scaling

Model details on GitHub

Scaling up or scaling down, every change costs your team output. See how much.

⬆ Scaling up

New people need months to ramp up, and every joiner sets their team back. Teams split as they grow, and coordination eats more time. Hire too fast and output per person collapses.

⬇ Scaling down

Layoffs and high turnover remove more output than headcount. Knowledge leaves with people, teams are merged, the atmosphere suffers and more people quit.

How it works, in plain words

New people need time

Nobody is fully productive on day one. Experienced hires need about half a year, juniors up to two years.

Teams need time together

A new team needs most of a year to really click. Every arrival or departure sets it back a little.

Bigger isn't automatically better

More people means more alignment. Past about ten people, a team gets slower, not faster.

Every exit has a hidden price

Know-how leaves with people, seats stay empty for months, and many departures drag down the mood of everyone who stays.
